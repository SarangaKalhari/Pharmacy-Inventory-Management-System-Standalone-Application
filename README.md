# 💊 Pharmacy Inventory Management System
A standalone desktop application designed to streamline pharmacy operations with comprehensive inventory management, sales processing, and expiry tracking capabilities.

## 📋 Overview
This Pharmacy Inventory Management System provides an all-in-one solution for managing medicine inventory, supplier relationships, sales transactions, and expiry monitoring.

This application provides a user-friendly Point of Sale (POS) experience for pharmacies, helping manage stock levels, track expiry dates, and monitor sales transactions.

## 🚀 Features

### 💊 Medicine Management

- Add, update, delete, and view medicines

- Track medicine name, brand, expiry date, quantity, and price

### 🏢 Supplier Management

- Manage supplier details

- Maintain supplier contact information

### 🧾 Sales & Billing System

- Record sales transactions

- Generate billing details

### ⏰ Expiry Alerts

- Highlight medicines nearing expiry

- Notify users about expired products

### 📊 Stock & Sales Reports

- View stock summaries

- Track sales history and reports

### 🔍 Search & Filter

- Search medicines quickly

- Filter sales records and inventory

### ⚠️ Low Stock Notifications

- Dashboard alerts for low stock items

- Expiry notification panel
